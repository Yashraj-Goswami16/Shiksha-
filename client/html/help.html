Help and support

Find answers to common questions about using the student portal.

How do I start an examination?

Open Browse Examinations, select an assessment and review the instructions before entering the examination room.

Can I pause an examination?

Follow the instructions displayed for your assessment. A timed examination may need to be completed in one session.

Where can I view my results?

Open Results from the navigation menu to review your completed assessments.

Who can help with account issues?

Contact your institution or examination administrator for account-related support.
